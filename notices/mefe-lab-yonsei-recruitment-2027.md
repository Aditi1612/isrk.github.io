## Recruitment Overview

Dr. **Kiseok Lee** is recruiting the first members for the **MEFE Lab (Microbiome Ecology &amp; Functional Engineering)** at **Yonsei University**, Department of Systems Biology, Seoul, Korea.

The lab will open in **March 2027**, and the recruitment process is beginning now during **September-October 2026**.

---

## About The Lab

The MEFE Lab will study how microbiomes maintain their functions in changing environments and use these principles to design robust microbiome functions.

The research will combine:

- Microbial ecology
- Modeling
- Artificial intelligence
- Synthetic biology

These approaches will be used to understand, design, and engineer robust microbiome functions for applications in **women's health**, especially the vaginal microbiome, and **environmental systems**, including soil greenhouse gas emissions.

## Open Positions

Dr. Lee is currently building a talent pool and collecting CVs for:

- **1-2 undergraduate researchers**
- **1-2 PhD students**

The first members will help shape both the science and culture of the lab. Rather than starting with a predefined project, the team will read papers together, discuss interesting questions, and develop project ideas collaboratively.
