## Opportunity Overview

A Korean startup IT company, **ARYAZITECH**, is collecting profiles from candidates with experience or interest in IT support. This is an **initial profile collection**. Candidates whose profiles match the requirements will be contacted directly and given further information about the opportunity.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Company | ARYAZITECH |
| Job type | Remote / work from home |
| Work arrangement | Part-time |
| Language | Basic Korean-language understanding is helpful |

## Visa Eligibility

Holders of the following visas may apply:

- **F-2**
- **F-5**
- **D-2**, with valid permission to work

Applicants are responsible for ensuring that their visa status and work permission allow them to perform the offered work.

## Job Responsibilities

The responsibilities may include:

1. Providing desktop and laptop technical support
2. Installing and configuring Windows 10 and Windows 11
3. Performing PC imaging, reimaging and software installation
4. Troubleshooting hardware and software issues
5. Installing and maintaining servers and IT equipment
6. Performing network connectivity checks and troubleshooting
7. Supporting switches, routers, access points, LAN and Wi-Fi
8. Performing data-center racking and stacking
9. Installing, replacing and decommissioning servers
10. Installing and testing fiber and network cables
11. Replacing faulty hardware and coordinating warranty service
12. Providing end-user support
13. Maintaining IT assets and inventory
14. Supporting Microsoft 365
15. Preparing daily and weekly work reports
16. Communicating with customers, vendors and internal IT teams

Some listed responsibilities may require access to physical equipment. Shortlisted candidates should confirm the precise work location and working arrangement with ARYAZITECH when further details are provided.
