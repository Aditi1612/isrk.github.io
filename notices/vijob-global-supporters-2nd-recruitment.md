## Recruitment Overview

**VIJOB Global Supporters** is back for its second round with the theme **"Connect with your people in Korea."**

The program invites foreign students living in Korea to share their study life, career preparation journey, and experiences with the VIJOB community. Participants will create inspiring content, take part in official missions, and engage with new features on the VIJOB app.

---

## Eligibility

The recruitment is open to:

- Around **20 foreign students living in Korea**, including undergraduate and graduate students
- Students who can stay in Korea during the program period
- Students who can attend both the offline orientation and the graduation ceremony

---

## Activities

Selected supporters will:

- Complete official VIJOB missions
- Create content for the VIJOB app community and social media channels
- Join the early access program for new features on the VIJOB app

---

## Benefits

Participants will receive:

- VIJOB Supporters welcome kit
- **KRW 100,000 CJ gift card**
- Official certificate of completion that can be listed on a resume
- Official Supporters badge on the VIJOB app profile
- Early access to new app features
- Extra perks and rewards for outstanding supporters

---

## Preferred Qualifications

Active users of the **VIJOB app community** are preferred. Applicants who post in the community will be given priority.

Community post writing tip:

1. Tap the **Life** tab at the bottom of the app.
2. Go to the community.
3. Tap the **Write** button at the bottom to post.

---

## Selection Schedule

The selection process will follow this order:

| Step | Process |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Document screening |
| 2 | Online interview |
| 3 | Final selection notice |
| 4 | Offline orientation |

Applicants who pass the document screening and final selection will be notified individually.
