## Recruitment Consultation Overview

**Samsung Display** will conduct online one-on-one recruitment consultations for international students for the **second half of 2026**.

This is an opportunity for interested international students to learn more about recruitment opportunities, ask questions directly, and receive consultation related to Samsung Display's hiring process.

---

## Consultation Schedule

| Date | Time | Format |
|------|------|--------|
| **August 31, 2026 (Monday)** | 1:00-5:00 PM | Online one-on-one consultation |
| **September 1, 2026 (Tuesday)** | 1:00-5:00 PM | Online one-on-one consultation |
